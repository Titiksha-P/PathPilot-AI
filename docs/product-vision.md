# Product Vision

## Product Positioning

**PathPilot AI is an explainable career decision and education planning system for students who are overwhelmed by generic advice, scattered information, and unclear next steps.**

The platform is not designed to merely recommend careers. It is designed to help a student:

1. understand which careers fit their profile,
2. understand why those careers fit,
3. compare alternatives,
4. identify missing capabilities,
5. and follow a realistic development plan.

## Core Problem

Students commonly receive career guidance through aptitude tests, social media, relatives, counsellors, or fragmented websites. These sources often create more confusion because they:

- provide generic suggestions,
- fail to explain recommendations,
- ignore constraints and preferences,
- do not compare alternatives,
- and stop before giving an actionable next step.

## Product Promise

> PathPilot AI turns career uncertainty into an explainable, comparable, and actionable decision journey.

## Primary User

A student who is choosing among education or career paths and needs structured guidance based on:

- interests,
- skills,
- preferred subjects,
- education level,
- career goals,
- learning preferences,
- constraints,
- and readiness to build missing capabilities.

## Winning Differentiator

### Career Decision Simulator

Instead of showing only a ranked list, PathPilot AI allows the student to compare two career paths through the lens of their own profile.

The comparison covers:

- personal fit,
- current skill alignment,
- missing skills,
- qualification requirements,
- time-to-readiness,
- learning effort,
- opportunity relevance,
- and recommended next actions.

## Product Principles

1. **Explain before persuading.** Every recommendation must include understandable reasons.
2. **Guide decisions, not browsing.** Every screen must move the student closer to a choice or action.
3. **Personalize with evidence.** Recommendations must use actual profile factors, not vague motivational language.
4. **Show trade-offs honestly.** Career comparison must reveal strengths, gaps, and effort.
5. **Convert insight into action.** Every recommendation must end with specific next steps.

## First-Demo Scope

The hackathon demo should prove one complete journey:

1. Student completes a short profile.
2. System generates the top three career matches.
3. Student opens the explanation for a recommendation.
4. Student compares two career paths.
5. System identifies skill gaps.
6. System generates a personalized 90-day roadmap.

This focused flow is more valuable than a large collection of disconnected features.
